# Python_Pete
Welcome to my corner of Python experimentation.
I am Peter, an octagenarian retiree who twenty years ago, practiced industrial engineering within an airport environment.
I dabble in Python because I find it fascinating and it helps to keep my mind active. 
I have no grand professional ambitions - just the joy of learning and staying connected with my grandsons through tech.
Perhaps I may even use the enormous potential of Python to revisit some of the challenging airport systems which I found 
difficult to model 20 years ago.
Therefore, this repository will be a mix of experiments, modelling, learning exercises and whatever sparks an old man's curiosity.
